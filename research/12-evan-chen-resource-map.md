## SECTION 2: OTIS UNIT CATALOG (~70 units)

### Algebra (15 units)
- **Alg Manip** (B/D) — Pure algebraic manipulation
- **Analysis** (D/Z) — Real analysis, convergence, Lagrange multipliers
- **Cyclotomic** (B) — Roots of unity, e^(iθ), trig connections
- **Formulas** (D/Z) — "Write Down Formulas" (Yang Liu MOP 2018), combinatorial recursions, NT power sums
- **Gen Func** (B) — Generating functions, snake oil
- **Hard Ineq** (Z) — Hardest inequality gems
- **Ineq Basic** (B) — AM-GM, homogenization, Cauchy/Hölder
- **Ineq Func** (B) — f(x₁)+...+f(xₙ) type: Jensen, Karamata, tangent line trick
- **Ineq Standard** (D) — Combined Ineq Basic + Ineq Func
- **Integration Bee** (D) — MIT-style integration problems
- **Irreducible** (D) — Polynomial irreducibility: F_p[x], Rouché, ad-hoc
- **Putnam Analysis** (Z) — Swapping infinite integrals
- **Real Polynom** (D/Z) — Vieta/Newton, multivariable, interpolation, differentiation
- **Sums** (B/D) — Switching order of summation, generating functions, Snake Oil
- **Symm Polynom** (B) — Vieta formulas, Newton sums, fundamental theorem. "Easiest unit"
- **Tricky Ineq** (D) — Problems that DON'T succumb to standard methods

### Combinatorics (22 units)
- **Adv Poly Method** (Z) — Combinatorial Nullstellensatz + GF on hard problems
- **Arrows** (D/Z) — Functions as directed graphs, cycle decomposition
- **C8 Summit** (Z) — Hardest combo problems, year-end capstone
- **Computational Combo** (B/D) — "ARML combo": Rigid+Induct+Formulas+Grinding. Black-magic bijections
- **Entry Combo** (B) — Beginner: induction, recursion, invariants, algorithms
- **Equality** (B/D/Z) — Exploiting equality cases. MANDATORY for newcomers
- **Expected Value** (B) — Probability, linearity of expectation, Markov chains
- **Extremal Graph** (D/Z) — Turán's theorem, maximizing edges avoiding cliques
- **Free** (D/Z) — Constructions, inventing math. Counterpart to Rigid
- **Global** (B/D) — Linearity of expectation, double counting, pigeonhole — "all the same idea"
- **Global and Local** (Z) — Accelerated version of both Global+Local
- **Graph Theory** (B/D/Z) — Graph theory at all levels
- **Grids** (Z) — Rectangular grid problems
- **Induction & Recursion I** (B) — Substantial induction/recursion
- **Induction & Recursion II** (D) — Harder duplicate of I
- **Intermediate Mix** (D) — Mixed combo at IMO 2/5 level
- **IOI** (D/Z) — Algorithmic/CS-flavored problems from IOI
- **Linear Algebra** (D/Z) — LA over ℝ and F₂ applied to combinatorics
- **Local** (D) — Perturbation, greedy algorithms. Counterpart to Global
- **Mystery** (B) — Secret topic (discovery-based)
- **Process** (D/Z) — Understanding moving processes (windmill-type). Like Rigid but more hostile
- **Rigid** (B/D/Z) — Discovering structure in fixed setups. "My favorite unit"
- **Russian Combo** (B/D) — Problems from Russia

### Functional Equations (4 units)
- **Func Eqn** (B/D/Z) — Standard FEs. Most completed FE unit (741 students)
- **Monster FE** (D/Z) — Non-trivial solution FEs (not just f(x)=x)
- **Real-to-Real Func Eqn** (D) — Cookie-cutter ℝ→ℝ FEs
- **Wrapped Func Eqn** (D) — All variables wrapped by function

### Geometry (21 units)
- **AIME Geo** (B/D) — Computational geometry, AIME tail-end
- **Art School** (D/Z) — DIAGRAM INTUITION: guessing which claims should be true. Pure meta-cognition
- **Bary** (B/D) — Barycentric coordinates follow-up to EGMO Ch7
- **Classical Geo** (D/Z) — Between Config and Elem: piecing together master diagrams
- **Complex Nums** (B/D) — Complex numbers follow-up to EGMO Ch6
- **Config Geo** (D/Z) — Synthetic problems building on standard configurations (USAMO 2016/3 style)
- **Constructor** (D) — Ruler-compass + exotic tool constructions. 1 student completed
- **Elem Geo** (B/D) — Basic tools: angle chasing, Power of Point, homothety. Follow-up to EGMO Part I
- **G6 Summit** (Z) — Hardest geometry, year-end capstone
- **Harmonic** (D) — Projective geometry: harmonic bundles, poles/polars. Follow-up to EGMO Ch9
- **Homography** (D/Z) — Abstract projective: transformations, conics, Pascal, DIT/DDIT
- **Hybrid Geo** (D/Z) — Geometry + algebra/combo/NT crossover. Geometric inequalities, lattice points
- **Hyperbola** (Z) — Rectangular circumhyperbolas, Poncelet point. For fun
- **Inversion and Spiral** (Z) — Harder follow-up to EGMO Ch8+10
- **Invert** (D) — Inversion follow-up to EGMO Ch8
- **Linear Power** (Z) — Linearity of difference of PoP, forgotten coaxiality lemma
- **Moving Points** (Z) — Projective animation technique. Contributed by Anant Mudgal
- **Spiral** (D) — Spiral similarity and Miquel points, follow-up to EGMO Ch10
- **Super Bary** (Z) — Hard barycentric coordinates
- **Super Complex** (Z) — Hard complex numbers
- **Trig and Lengths** (D) — Trig-bash: chasing lengths/ratios with trig
- **Weird Geo** (D/Z) — Pentagons, hexagons, counting degrees of freedom

### Number Theory (13 units)
- **Analytic NT** (Z) — Asymptotic calculations, multiplicative NT, convolution
- **Euclid Alg** (D) — Bezout, Euclidean algorithm, "remainder bounding"
- **Expon NT** (D) — aⁿ±1: mods, orders, Fermat Christmas, LTE
- **Heavy NT** (D) — Vieta jumping, quadratic reciprocity, big theorems
- **Int Polynom** (D/Z) — ℤ[x] theory, algebraic integers, irreducibility, bordering algebraic NT
- **Misc NT** (D) — Miscellaneous NT
- **Mod Arith 1: Proofs** (B) — Modular arithmetic, proof-based
- **Mod Arith 2: Computation** (B) — Modular arithmetic, computational
- **N7 Summit** (Z) — Hardest NT, year-end capstone
- **NT Construct** (D/Z) — Construction problems in NT (like Free but with ℤ structure)
- **Orders** (B) — Orders mod prime, AIME/JMO border
- **Prime Exponents** (B/D) — ν_p usage in olympiad problems
- **Size in NT** (D) — Using size/magnitude to handle NT conditions
- **Super NT** (Z) — Expert NT practice combining Exp+Heavy+other

### Higher Math (4 units)
- **Game Theory** — Nash equilibrium, subgame perfect, Bayesian
- **Groups, Rings, and Fields** — Gateway to abstract algebra
- **Multivariable Calculus** — Based on Evan's MIT 18.02 LAMV textbook

### Miscellaneous (5 units)

1. OTIS Excerpts PDF (202 problems, walkthroughs with "bogus solutions" showing anti-patterns)
2. Olympiad Inequalities handout (decision tree for inequality technique selection)
3. Introduction to Functional Equations handout (substitution motivation framework)
4. Lemmas in AoPS Geometry handout (named configuration catalog)
5. Some Advice for Olympiad Geometry blog post (three-method framework)

**Tier 2 — Solution notes with motivation remarks:**
6. IMO solution notes 2020-2025 (most recent, most relevant patterns)
7. USAMO solution notes 2020-2025
8. TSTST solutions 2020-2024

**Tier 3 — OTIS unit descriptions (technique taxonomy):**
9. The OTIS catalog itself provides the most systematic classification of competition math into ~70 technique-organized units. Each unit description tells you WHAT the unit teaches and WHY problems are grouped together — this IS a pattern taxonomy.

**Tier 4 — Blog posts on approach selection:**
10. Hard and Soft Techniques
11. Getting to Know Problems
12. On Reading Solutions
13. Math Contest Platitudes v3

**Tier 5 — Textbooks:**
14. EGMO (geometry technique recognition throughout)
